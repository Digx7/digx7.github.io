A game I made in Unity for the GMTK 2025 Game Jam

Below is the marketing copy found on the games page



PUT IN FULL SCREEN FOR BEST RESULTS

The world is frozen and you now control the last remnants of society surviving on a train.  Will you lead humanity to salvation or watch it all crumble?

============================================

Controls:

Move - A/D or Arrows

Interact - Left Click

============================================

Tips:

You can inspect any train car by clicking on it.

To buy a new train car click on the + icon behind the last train car.

Most train cars need workers, assign them by click on a train car and clicking on the '+'.

Each Worker and Engineer will consume 1 Meal per turn and will require 1 Lodging.

Farms produce Raw Food, Kitchens turn those into Meals.

To win max out Hope.

Hope is ONLY raised by the Entertainment Car

Discontent can ONLY be slowed down with the Prison Car

============================================

Known Bugs:

If you want to try again refresh the browser page (will not work other wise)
