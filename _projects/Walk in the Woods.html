Take your dog out for a casual walk in the woods.  Enjoy the sights.  What could go wrong?




----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Controls:

Action ----------[Keyboard]

Move -----------WASD 

Jump------------Space

Swap ----------- E 

Interact -------- Q



----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This game was made for the GMTK 2021 game jam which took place from June 11th to June 13th

We have since then taken a month to polish up the game into what you see today
