This is the racing game of the future.  Build, Design, and Race your way to the finish line.  Completely customize the way your ship looks and handles from the ground up using the parts at your disposal.  Then go out a race to get new and better parts.  Design your ship around the challenge and let the challenge get you better ships.


Controls :

(Ship)

Turn left/right ______________ A/D

Rotate up/down ____________W/S

Accelerate _________________Space

Drift ______________________Shift


This game is made as a side project for fun and my have bugs.


This game was made for the JAC Fullerton Connect Expo to be showcased by the VGDC club and takes inspiration from Wipeout 2048 and F-Zero.
