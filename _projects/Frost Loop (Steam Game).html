You can find the game here: Link

Below is the marketing copy found on the games steam store page



Frost Loop is a city building survival game set on a train in the snow.  Inspired by FrostPunk and SnowPiercer.




You start with a train car, a few people, meager resources, and must build up to the ultimate train to bring humanity through the frozen apocalypse.


Build

Build up your train one car at a time.  Each train car generates its own resource and can be destroyed.  You can only build so many train cars, so which will you build to survive?


Manage

Manage your people and resources.  Will you have what you need to keep the train rolling?


Pilot

Pilot your train around the map to avoid the always moving storms and to uncover key locations.
