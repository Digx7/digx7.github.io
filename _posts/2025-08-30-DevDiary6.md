## Friday 9/12

Spent the day trying out a new devlog recording process and working on UI for the trains in the game

### Devlog recording process

I'm trying to record my mic as I work that way I can get my audio narration as I go instead of needing to record again after the fact.
This can save me time as I can record my thoughts as they are fresh.
It may affect the video qualities, but to be far I'm not putting out videos at the moment anyway dispite wanting to.
So maybe this can get started regularly posting these as videos

### UI for the trains in the game

I got it so you can press a button on the HUD to show some info about each train car above it.
This info includes: Health, Warmth, and Workforce (*workers and engineers*).

## Saturday 9/13

Fixed bug with buffs, updated the map scene camera, added map space ui, worked on objectives

### Bug with buffs

Was having a bug where the buffs weren't unloading correctly.  I fixed it

### Map Scene Camera

It now loads up focused on the players location

### Map Space UI

You can now toggle on and off showing the tempature and snow level of any given space

### Objectives

Worked on the backend system and UI of adding objectives to the game
