Once I finished the art I bundled all of them up and sold them as assets of various game asset stores.

In that process I had to make a thumbnail image for those assets.  For the thumbnail I turned to a new drawing tool.

InkScape.

InkScape lets you create Vector Art images.  Vector art has the upside being able to scale up and down to any size without losing quality.

I needed 6 different sized thumbnails across all store pages.  Rather than make 6 different images, I could create 1 image in InkScape and easily export it to the 6 different sizes I needed, without losing image quality.

Inkscape was a bit of a learning curve, but I will likely use it in the future for similar situations.
